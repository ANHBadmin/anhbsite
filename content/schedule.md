SUNRISE SUMMIT AT HUNTCLIFF  
8592 Roswell Road, Sandy Springs, GA 30350 – Lower Terrace  
(Intersection Hightower Trail & Roswell Road)  
Landmark: 3 Dollar Café  
Standard Uniform: White band/Polo shirt, black
pants/skirts, black socks and shoes  
Parking:  
Enter through the main entrance from Roswell Road, across from Three Dollar Café.  
Immediately keep to the left to go around the building.  
As you come around you will see 1 or 2 white busses. Stop there and look to the right where the
terrace and our band stand are.  
You can unload there and if there is space, park too. But we should give preference to those
with walkers or other assistance needs as well as heavy instruments. From there goes a paved
trail where heavy equipment can be rolled up to the band stand.  
If there are free spaces you can park in the lot to the left. Otherwise go back to the front and
look for parking.  
Returning, those going south toward Sandy Springs or GA400 via Northridge Drive exit main
entrance from where you can only turn right.  
Those going north toward Roswell need to cross the front door to the left and go down a narrow
drive, turn right at the end and right into Hightower Trail to the traffic light and turn left.  
Got lost, call Wolfgang 404-697-8960
